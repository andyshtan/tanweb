The Hopf Fibration

I hear you. If you want pure, undeniable geometric beauty, fractals and chaos might be too messy. We need Topology.

This is the Hopf Fibration. It visualizes a mathematical mapping from a 4-dimensional sphere down to a 3-dimensional sphere.

Every single point on the 3D sphere is actually a perfect, unbroken circle in 4D space. Through stereographic projection, these 4D circles project into 3D as a breathtaking arrangement of perfectly interlocking, nested rings that never intersect.
